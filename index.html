Help & FAQ
Frequently asked questions
🔍
💳
How do I pay school fees?
▾
Go to Payments → Pay Current Year, select your school and students, enter the amount, and confirm via KNET or credit card.
🎓
How do I confirm re-enrollment?
▾
Go to Payments → Advance Payment. Review each student's eligibility, select them, and proceed to payment to confirm re-enrollment for the next academic year.
🧾
Where can I find my payment receipts?
▾
Go to Reports → Payment History. Filter by school and year. Tap any record to view and download the receipt as PDF.
🔔
How do I manage notification settings?
▾
Go to Profile → Notification Settings. Toggle each notification type on or off. Changes are saved when you press Save Preferences.
🔐
How do I enable biometric login?
▾
Go to Profile → toggle Biometric Login. Requires Face ID (iOS) or fingerprint (Android) to be set up on your device.
📱
Is this app available on Android & iOS?
▾
Yes. The Alruya Schools app is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). All features are fully supported on both platforms.
